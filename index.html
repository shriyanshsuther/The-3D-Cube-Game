    How to play
    
    
    Twist the layers until every face of the cube is a single colour. The timer starts on your first move.
    
    
    Turn a layer
    
    
    Drag across a face of the cube. Drag on the empty background to rotate the whole cube and see the other sides.
    
    
    Keyboard
    
    
    
      
    U D L R F B
    Turn the up, down, left, right, front or back face. Hold Shift to turn it the other way.
    
      
    Arrow keys
    Rotate the whole cube
    
      
    Z / Y
    Undo / redo
    
      
    P or Esc
    Pause or resume
    
      
    Space
    Start from the menu
    
      
    M / F / H
    Sound / fullscreen / this help
    
    
    
    
    Daily challenge
    
    
    Everyone gets the same scramble each day (UTC). Your best time for each cube size is saved on this device.
